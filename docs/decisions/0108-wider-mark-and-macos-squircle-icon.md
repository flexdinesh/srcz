# 0108: Wider mark and macOS squircle icon

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-08
- Revises: [0104](0104-source-zero-logo.md)'s mark geometry and its one
  desktop raster for every OS

## Context

The mark read narrow: its Z took 20 of 32 units across and 28 down. On macOS 26
the desktop icon was worse: Tahoe sets any app icon that is not its squircle on
a grey squircle plate, so the square graphite tile sat shrunk inside a grey
frame. A full-bleed square still protrudes past the squircle's corners and gets
the same plate.

## Decision

**The mark is 20% wider.** The Z spans 24 of 32 units (12 of 16 on the small
grid) at the same height. The diagonal keeps its stroke weight and the rails
keep their width and gap, so only the proportion changes:

| Grid | Z | Rails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 32 | `M4 2H28V8.5L14 23.5H28V30H4V23.5L18 8.5H4z` | `M4 9.5H8V18.12L4 22.4zM28 22.5H24V13.88L28 9.6z` |
| 16 | `M2 1H14V4L8 12H14V15H2V12L8 4H2z` | `M2 5H4V8.45L2 11.12zM14 11H12V7.55L14 4.88z` |

Every copy carries it: `BrandMark`, the web favicon, the site's `Mark`,
favicon, and docs logos, and every master in `docs/brand/`. The lockups keep
the 8-unit gap before the wordmark and widen their viewBox to 164.

**macOS gets its own icon.** `docs/brand/tile-macos.svg` fills Apple's
squircle in graphite: an 824 body at a 100 inset of 1024, drawn with continuous
corners (radius 185.4), with the white Z and coral rails. `macos.icon` in
`apps/desktop/mygo.json` points mygo at `apps/desktop/resources/icon.icns`
instead of the `.icns` it would render from `icon.png`. Linux keeps the square
tile in `icon.png`, so square corners (0102) stay wherever the OS allows them.

Rasters stay committed and regenerated by hand:

```sh
rsvg-convert -w 1024 -h 1024 docs/brand/tile.svg -o apps/desktop/resources/icon.png
node docs/brand/icns.mjs apps/desktop/resources/icon.icns
rsvg-convert -w 1200 -h 630 docs/brand/og.svg -o apps/site/public/og.png
```

`icns.mjs` renders the macOS master with `rsvg-convert` and packs the PNGs
under the entry types mygo writes (ic07 to ic14, without icp4 and icp5).

## Consequences

- The macOS icon has rounded corners; the square-corner rule now covers the
  Linux icon, the web, and the site only.
- Tahoe's plate check is not documented. The squircle follows Apple's template
  but has only been checked against renders, not on a Mac.
- The `.icns` is another committed binary to regenerate whenever the mark
  changes.
